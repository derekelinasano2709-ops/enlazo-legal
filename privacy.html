Enlazo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Enlazo handles information when you interact with our Discord bot.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Enlazo may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, game scores, levels, streaks, achievements, game activity, and commands submitted to the bot.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate games, maintain player progress, calculate rankings, provide rewards, respond to commands, and improve the service.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be stored to support persistent features. Information is retained only as needed for the bot's operation, subject to technical limitations and applicable requirements. Actual storage and deletion practices depend on the bot's implementation.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and the hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate Enlazo. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your use of Enlazo or wish to request its deletion, contact the Enlazo team through the official support channel, if available. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

8. Children's Privacy

Enlazo is not intended to collect personal information directly from children outside the ordinary operation of Discord. Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the bot changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Enlazo team through the official support channel, if available.
